# Plan 01 summary: five facts a reader disputed, tested on the G2 and corrected

## Why

On 2026-11-02 a reader on the HP forum reported five things in
`docs/topics/ppl.md` as wrong or misleading, and questioned the reference as a
source of truth for a model. Reading the evidence behind them showed the same
fault each time: one observation or one table row with no program, written down
as a rule, against `CONTRIBUTING.md`'s "one failure is not a rule". The re-tests
of 2026-09-24 had gone around those claims and not through them.

## How it was tested

On a G2 with firmware 2.4.15515, on 2026-11-05, over USB, at the user's approval:
send a program, type its name on Home, read the answer from the screen. That is
now `usb.compile-check`. A program that compiles answers its number, and one that
does not leaves its name unknown, so Home reads it as a product of letters. The
controls came out as they must: `ZCOK` 4700, `ZCBAD` with `ENDIF` read as
`Z*C*B*A*D` and 0, `ZCOKB` 4702.

`hpprime send` runs the linter first, and the linter's own rules were under test,
so a scratch runner sent with the same code and without it; `hpprime send --no-lint`
does that now, and the runner stayed out of the repository. The programs are in
`probes/`. The screens are in `programs/_g2-review-2026-11-05/`, which git ignores.

## What changed

- **`hpprime lint`.** `local-first` removed. `export-multiple` is now `export-limit`,
  an error from 9. New: `local-scope`, and `export-home-variable`, an error for `Y`
  and `Z` and a warning labelled `unverified` for the calculator's other variables.
  Twenty-one rules. Run over `examples/`, `templates/`, `programs/` and the
  Connectivity Kit's folders, 90 files, the three rules that fire on `EXPORT` and
  on a `LOCAL`'s block found nothing outside the deliberately wrong programs of this
  review.
- **`hpprime run`.** Reads a forward declaration. Refuses to load 9 initialised
  `EXPORT`s, an `EXPORT` of `Y` or `Z`, and a `LOCAL` read after its block, and takes
  7 and 8. Raises "not covered" on exporting another of the calculator's variables,
  and on a nested `LOCAL` that repeats an outer one. A block's `LOCAL`s now leave the
  frame when it ends. Every G2 answer above is its answer now, but for `TICKS`,
  which it does not cover.
- **`hpprime send`.** Unchanged, and it no longer refuses a `LOCAL` after other
  statements or 7 or 8 `EXPORT`s, because the linter does not.
- **Documentation.** `ppl.md`, `usb.md`, `AGENTS.md`, the paste block in
  `docs/ai/prompts.md`, step 2 of the guided path, `tools.md`, the entries `LOCAL`,
  `EXPORT`, `BEGIN`, `GETKEY`, `TICKS` and `Entry`, and the counts in the README: 141
  facts, 95 on a G2. Two lines of `LOCAL.md` that were already stale, `7 or 8` and
  `i` and `e`, went with them. `examples/selftest/SELFTEST.txt` and
  `examples/keymap/` still tell a person to leave the parentheses out, which holds in
  the two modes that fail, so they stay as they are.

Suite 43,711 passed, 0 failed. `hpprime docs --check`: 0 problems.

## What is left

- **The emulator.** The reply on the forum promises these on the emulator too. It
  takes `hpprime examples --compile` on the programs here and somebody pressing
  `Check`, plus `F()` typed on its Home. Nothing here was run on it.
- **Asking that reader** which entry mode and firmware `F()` works on for them. The report
  did not reproduce in `Entry` 0 or 1.
- **Not measured, and said so in the facts:** `A` to `X` and the numbered variables
  exported; a function called like one of them, which `EXPORT F()` in the tests is;
  how many one `EXPORT` holds with no values; a loop's or a `CASE`'s `LOCAL`, a
  `THEN`'s read in its `ELSE`, and a nested `LOCAL` repeating an outer one; lists
  timed.
- **`MAKEMAT` refused 150x150 and 200x200** with "memoria insuficiente", while
  100x100 worked, once and with a 100x100 still held. One failure is not a rule, so
  it is not in the documentation.
- **The other facts that rest on one observation**, which the reply on the forum also
  promises to mark `unverified` until tested the same way. A first pass over the
  evidence: 9 in `ppl.md` say only "recorded in a table", 5 of them re-measured in
  part, and about 20 other `G2` facts claim that something fails and have no row of
  their own. That is a filter, and each needs reading.
