# Plan 02 summary: the review before the commit

## Why

Plan 01 found one failure with several faces: a claim outran its evidence, and then
the kit's own machinery enforced and repeated it. The tree held work from several
sessions, and it was about to go public, so before the commit it was read as a whole,
looking for that shape everywhere and giving plan 01 no credit for being recent.

## What it found in plan 01 itself

- **`Y`** was refused by the linter and the interpreter as measured. It had only ever
  been exported beside `Z`, and `Z` alone explained that failure.
- **`local-scope`** was an ERROR, and `hpprime run` said "does not compile on the
  calculator", for every kind of block, when the fact said only an `IF` was measured.
- **`hpprime run` answered** a global read after a nested `LOCAL` of its name with a
  test saying "not measured, but not in doubt".
- **`hpprime send` could not send what the linter refuses**, so a false lint error
  could not be tested through the kit: the machinery protected the claim.
- **`Y` and `Z` were written twice**, in `lint.py` and `interp.py`.

All of them turned out either wrong or unproven in the way the review expected, and
the measurements below settle them. Every number of plan 01 matched its screenshot.

## What changed

- **Facts.** `ppl.local-block-scope` covers every block. `ppl.export-initialised` is 8
  variables, with values or not. `ppl.export-home-variable` covers `A`, `Y` and `Z`,
  and what a function called `F` does. `ppl.global-namespace`, `ppl.compilation-order`,
  `ppl.index-call`, `ppl.no-end-keywords`, `ppl.expr-empty`, `ppl.expr-dynamic-access`
  and `ppl.local-limit` rest on programs now, where they rested on "recorded in a
  table" or on nothing.
- **Labels.** Seven `G2` facts whose evidence named no program and no answer are
  `unverified`, and say what was seen: `ppl.decimal-point`, `apps.exports-tied`,
  `interface.input-modal`, `interface.offscreen-grob`, `micropython.modules`,
  `micropython.string-quotes`, `micropython.bridge-cost`. `format.md` says what a label
  promises, and `hpprime docs --check` fails on `G2` evidence that only says it was
  measured or recorded in a table.
- **Linter.** `local-scope` walks the blocks and is an error wherever measured,
  `REPEAT` and `IFERR` included. `export-limit` counts variables, with values or not.
  `export-home-variable` is an error for `A`, `Y`, `Z`, and warns on `EXPORT F()`, since
  on Home `F` is the variable. `export-clash` with `--set` is a warning: the calculator
  takes both, and an error is only what it was seen to refuse. Twenty-three rules.
- **Interpreter.** Follows every answer above, nested `LOCAL`s included. A test holds
  its list of what cannot be exported to the linter's.
- **`hpprime send`.** `--no-lint` asks the calculator about a rule of the linter, so
  `probes/g2probe.py` left the repository. Its message about closing the Kit says to
  ask first. A guard refusing names on HP's list was written and taken out the same
  day: it refused the guided path's own `CIRCLE`, for `circle` in the Geometry app, and
  an app's name is not on that list anyway, so it protected nothing it claimed to.
- **Hooks.** `hpkit/gates.py` takes the kit from the event's `cwd`, so a session in a
  worktree is checked against its own `programs/` and facts. README, `AGENTS.md` and
  `tools.md` say what has been tried, and that they do not run where only `python3`
  starts.
- **Merged.** `claude/amazing-darwin-20427b`: `interface.wait-events`, `wait-touch`,
  `examples/touch/`, themes, screen capacity, `apps.main-py`, number notation.
  `claude/competent-lalande-3b6a4e`: `interface.font-glyphs`, `font-glyph`,
  `examples/glyphs/`. Both uncommitted there, brought in three-way, branches untouched.
  The probes behind the 2026-10-04 facts, which lived only in that worktree's ignored
  `programs/`, are in `probes/emulator-2026-10/`.
- **The suite's number.** `test_docs.py` walked `.claude/worktrees/`, so the 43,711 of
  plan 01 counted two more copies of the documentation, and a stray worktree could
  fail it. It keeps out of `.claude/` now, and the suite is about 15,300 checks.
- **Smaller.** The personal path in `12-03-PLAN.md` and `11-03-SUMMARY.md`; the
  forum reader's name, not asked; `examples/README.md` telling people to run `SELF3()`;
  `EXPR.md` still saying `Check` names only the last bad line; `format.md`'s sample
  still saying 7 or 8; `F` as the example name in `AGENTS.md` and the tools' usage,
  since it does not work from Home; the `hpprime` launcher's executable bit, left out
  on 2026-09-29 and now set.
